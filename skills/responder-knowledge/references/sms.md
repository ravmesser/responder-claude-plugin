# SMS (שליחת SMS)

Send SMS campaigns to lists, with a virtual number for tracking replies.

## Send a new SMS campaign (איך שולחים דיוור SMS חדש)
**Prerequisite:** a list with recipients (who have phone numbers).
1. Toolbar → **"דיוורים / קמפיינים" (Campaigns)** → **"דיוור חדש" (New Campaign)**.
2. Enter an internal name.
3. Choose **"SMS"** as the type → **"הבא" (Next)**.
4. **Select recipients:** add one or more lists (duplicates removed automatically), or
   filter by behaviour/tenure/tags/custom fields; you can also exclude lists.
5. **SMS settings:** tags; **sender number** (personal profile or the virtual number);
   delivery time (now or scheduled); option "remove email when removing phone".
6. **Write the message:** Hebrew ≈ **201 characters**, English ≈ **160 characters**
   (spaces and merge fields count; final count depends on recipient device).
7. **"שמור ושלח ניסיון" (Save & send test)** to preview, or **"שמור וצא" (Save & exit)**.
